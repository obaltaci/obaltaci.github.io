Content Hub

A private publishing tool by Omer Baltaci.

What it is

Content Hub is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It helps make short videos and picture carousels for the owner's own channels (the Curiosity Lab) and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

It is not a public service. It has one user - its owner - and it does not offer sign-up, accounts or any service to other people.

How it uses Google and YouTube

When the owner connects the tool to YouTube, Google asks the owner to allow two things, and the tool asks for nothing else:

  • Upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (the youtube.upload permission).
  • View the owner's YouTube account, to confirm which channel is connected and whether an upload has finished processing (the youtube.readonly permission).

The tool does not read anybody else's data, and the permission can be taken back at any time from the owner's Google account (Security → Third-party access).
